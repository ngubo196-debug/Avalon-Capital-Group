About Us

Plumbline Plumbing Services CC was established in 2009 and has been providing quality, professional and reliable plumbing services to our valued customers throughout KwaZulu-Natal. We pride ourselves on our commitment to excellence, integrity and customer satisfaction in everything that we do.

Our mission is to exceed our customers expectations by delivering world-class plumbing solutions at competitive prices. Our team of dedicated professionals is fully committed to providing the highest standard of workmanship on every project, big or small. No job is too big or too small for us!
